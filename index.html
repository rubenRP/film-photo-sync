Blix

Match film scans to iPhone time and place.

Film frames have no clock. Blix copies timestamps and GPS from iPhone photos onto your scans so they sort and map correctly in Apple Photos. Everything stays in this browser.

How it works

  1. 01

    Upload

    Add iPhone photos from the same period as the roll, plus your film scans (JPEG or TIFF).

  2. 02

    Anchors

    Pair the first and last frames with iPhone shots so Blix knows when the roll started and ended.

  3. 03

    Timeline

    Match remaining frames on the iPhone timeline. Unmatched photos get interpolated times between their neighbors.

  4. 04

    Rename

    Optionally set a filename prefix and starting number before export.

  5. 05

    Export

    Download a ZIP with timestamps and GPS written into each scan.

Client-side only. Photos never leave this device.
